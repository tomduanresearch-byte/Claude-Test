## 2026-09-23: Second meeting

**Publisher's notes (verbatim):** "This isnt great. I really dont even like the premise - theres no charm theres no ambition, it feels like a slice of life novel just from the prompt (ure making the world feel constrained and theres no driving force). Fundamentally, cultivation, battle, progression."

**My reading of the notes:** *The Pill Taster* was small and inward: one sect, a murder mystery and a critique of the system. The publisher wants the genre's core pleasures up front: a protagonist with big ambition and charm, a vast and dangerous world with heights worth climbing, a strong driving force, frequent fights, and progression through the realms that the reader can feel. The rejection is accepted. The old premise is retired, not defended.

**Delivered (new book: *Heaven's Debtor*, Book One: *Dust and Thunder*):**
- `novel/pitch.md`: replaced. Title, hook, logline, protagonist Ling Xiao and his goals, the Debt cultivation system (Eat, Owe, Pay), how progression feels, selling points, comparable titles, audience.
- `novel/bible.md`: replaced. A vertical world of the Dust and Nine Heavens (Heaven-pressure ties realm to altitude), the realm ladder and tribulation counts, the full Debt rules and Arc One progression table, factions, characters, places, glossary, author-only series mysteries, style notes.
- `novel/outline.md`: replaced. Arc One in 15 chapters, each with a fight, breakthrough or heist and a hook; Ling Xiao goes from QC 3 to Foundation Establishment. Sketches of Arcs Two and Three.
- `novel/chapters/01-put-it-on-my-tab.md`: new sample Chapter 1, about 3,100 words. It includes a street fight, a Core Formation tribulation, the theft of the ninth bolt, a breakthrough from QC 3 to QC 6, and ends on the Warden's eyes finding him.
- `novel/chapters/01-the-taster.md`: deleted (git history keeps it).

**Questions put to the publisher:**
1. Is this the right level of heat for the protagonist's cockiness, or should he be even brasher?
2. Pace: the outline gets him to Foundation in 15 chapters. Should the climb be faster, with a breakthrough every few chapters, or should I let each realm breathe longer?
3. Mei Canglan: keep her as a rival-ally with her own agenda, or would the market prefer a romance thread planted early?
