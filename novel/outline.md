# Outline: Thunder-Born

## Arc One: Dust and Thunder (Chapters 1–15, about 45,000 words)

**Arc question:** Can a boy whose body only eats lightning climb out of the Dust, and what is waiting above the land everyone there calls Heaven?
**Arc shape:** From the red stakes of the Anvil to the Verdant Heaven. Ling Xiao eats his first bolt and finds out he's thunder-born. He gets hunted by a Warden, a swordswoman and a gang, and eats blood-lightning in a fallen shard of a broken heaven. He climbs the Skygate Pillar and is caught by an old elder who wants to refine him into a pill. He forces the elder's own tribulation down on the Pillar and eats his way into Foundation Establishment, then gives the swordswoman's bolt back. He ends the arc standing on "Heaven" and finding out how much more sky there is.
**How the world opens in Arc One:** Ch. 1 the Dust's view (one green land overhead; songs of nine heavens; Canglan falls from *above* it). Ch. 3 Canglan names what he is. Ch. 5–6 the Ruin is a shard of a heaven that broke, and the sky-map shows the Verdant Heaven as a thumbnail. Ch. 8 Bai is a small officer of a Court she has never seen. Ch. 15 the Verdant Heaven's own sky is crowded with lands; Bai's whisper of the True Realms; the glimpse.
**Clock:** The Skygate Climb opens 31 days after Ch. 1. Canglan's flaw sets in about 90 days. The lodged sword-thunder slowly cuts at him.
**Power range:** Ling Xiao goes from QC 3 to Foundation Establishment (early). Enemies range from QC 5 (gang) through QC 9 (rival, gang boss) and Foundation (steward, Elder Hu) to Core and Nascent Soul (Canglan, Scarlet Lamp, Warden Bai), who can't be beaten, only outplayed or survived.
**Rule of thumb:** every chapter has a fight, a breakthrough or a heist.

### Chapter 1: The Sky Has No Top *(drafted)*
Nobody in Thunderfall knows how high the sky goes, and Ling Xiao means to find out. He's the crater baby, stuck at QC 3 because qi won't stay in him. He explains his plan to Dumpling: the Skygate token costs 100 stones, a thunder-heart sells for 300, and a sword cultivator's ninth bolt forks toward metal. He drops Big Tan of the Iron Crows into hollow glass. Mei Canglan of the Cloudsea Sword Sect falls out of the empty blue *above* Heaven's rim, which no candidate has ever done, and takes her Core Formation tribulation. In Heaven's breath Ling Xiao sprints two hundred paces inside the stakes and plants a sky-iron spike. The fork hits him and doesn't burn him. It feeds him. He breaks from QC 3 to QC 6 in three breaths, and most of the fork lodges in his belly like a cold sword. Under its taste is an older one he somehow knows. **Hook:** the Warden reads out "Nine bolts fell. The candidate received eight," and her eyes stop on him.

### Chapter 2: Eight Bolts and a Liar
Warden Bai questions the picker line. Ling Xiao lies to a Nascent Soul cultivator's face, and does it well: a stray fork hit his spike and knocked him flat. Her scroll reads him as QC 6, which no picker should be. She lets him go with a look that says she's counting. Canglan says nothing. Home to the Guo dumpling shop and Auntie Guo, who notices he ate four bowls and is still hungry, and not for dumplings. That night Big Tan and six Crows corner him for the thunder-heart. He reaches for the cold thing in his belly and a sword-arc tears out of his hand, cuts an iron pole in half, and opens his own forearm. He wins, but half the alley saw lightning come out of him. **Hook:** next morning, Mei Canglan is sitting in the Guo shop eating dumplings with her cracked sword across her knees. "You have something of mine."

### Chapter 3: A Sword in the Dumpling Shop
Canglan's bargain, told sparingly. The fork of her ninth bolt is lodged in him, whole enough to complete her core if he gives it back, and he can only channel a Core bolt out through Foundation meridians. Her flaw sets in about ninety days. So she will keep him alive, and he'll reach Foundation fast. The Dust is too thin, so he has to climb the Skygate. She tests him and gives him a word: *thunder-born*. Cloudsea has records of Thunder-Womb Bodies, and they all ended up in someone's pill furnace. Also dropped in passing: Cloudsea is "a long way above your Heaven," and she has never bothered to count the lands between. Training on the Anvil means her beating him flat, over and over. At dusk he lands one touch by leading her onto hollow glass, and she almost smiles. **Hook:** the Thunder Court posts a bounty on every wall in Thunderfall: *Bolt-thief. Qi Condensation. 1,000 stones.* Every gang in town is reading it.

### Chapter 4: The Ghost Market
Ling Xiao sells the thunder-heart in the lava tubes under the Anvil to buy two Skygate tokens, his and Dumpling's. He meets Hu Qingshan, a cold QC 9 spear fighter buying a spear head, who doesn't bother to hide his contempt for "picker luck." Steward Liang of Skygate Pavilion wins the heart at auction. In the tunnels afterward two bounty hunters (QC 9 and Foundation early) come for the thousand stones. Ling Xiao survives the first on nerve and terrain. Canglan kills the second at the last possible moment ("You were doing fine. Barely."). **Hook:** Liang holds the heart to the lamp and his smile goes still. Something has *fed* on this lightning, and he knows from his alchemy texts what feeds on lightning. He sends word up the Pillar to Elder Hu: *A Thunder-Womb Body, in our Dust.*

### Chapter 5: Blood Lightning in the Ruin
Canglan says QC 6 won't survive the upper Skygate. Ling Xiao says then he'd better eat. Uncle Guo, a smuggler, knows of an illegal tribulation in the Ruin: Daoist Scarlet Lamp of the Blood Lamp Sect, going for Core. Crossing to the Ruin, the Dust myth ("a god's bones") meets Canglan's flat correction: it's a shard of the Sundered Heaven, which broke long ago and fell on a thousand lands. "Your Heaven? Your Heaven is whole. This fell from much higher." They slip past the Blood Lamp guards (fight). Ling Xiao eats bolts two and three, blood-red and hungry, which are early and light enough. QC 7. He goes for the fourth out of greed and it nearly kills him, and that's how he learns his appetite has a ceiling. Scarlet Lamp forms a seven-bolt core, alive and furious. **Hook:** sheltering in an upside-down palace, Ling Xiao's torch finds a wall carved as a map of the sky from high above. It shows hundreds of lands. Near the bottom edge, small as a thumbnail, is the Verdant Heaven. At the top is a carved line, and one bolt of lightning drawn coming *down* through it, above a character neither of them can read.

### Chapter 6: What Fell From Higher
Scarlet Lamp, now Core and enraged, hunts them through the Ruin: qi-warped beasts, palaces hanging from what used to be the ground, a chase across an upside-down city. Ling Xiao learns to ride the blood-vein by feeding it once and then refusing it. Canglan takes a wound protecting him and hates that she did. They trade insults like two people becoming friends. They escape with a rubbing of the sky-map and a floatstone relic worth more than Thunderfall. **Hook:** back in Thunderfall, the Guo shop is smashed, a crow is painted on the shutters, and Dumpling is gone.

### Chapter 7: Crow's Nest
A raid on the Iron Crows' old kiln fortress. The Big Tan rematch lasts three moves, which shows the reader how far Ling Xiao has come. Then Crow-Father Gou (QC 9, iron claws): a hard, ugly fight in a burning kiln. Ling Xiao drives the blood-vein harder than is safe, digests the last of the blood-lightning mid-fight, and breaks to QC 8. Dumpling is rescued; Auntie Guo is safe with the neighbours and furious at everyone. In Gou's office are payment slips stamped with Steward Liang's seal, and an order: *take the crater baby alive.* **Hook:** Warden Bai steps out of the smoke.

### Chapter 8: The Warden's Arithmetic
A chase over the rooftops and onto the Anvil, a Nascent Soul against QC 8. He can't win or outrun her, so he outthinks her: he runs into the red circle of a *sanctioned* tribulation, where the Court's own law forbids her to interfere, and survives the stray lightning on the edge without eating a single bolt, in front of her. From the stakes she tells him the Court's doctrine: stolen lightning cracks heavens, and that's what broke the Ruin's parent. She also tells him, without meaning to say so much, that she is a Warden of a small Registry, that the Court's seat is somewhere she has never been, and that his birth is in her files: a Clear-Sky Bolt, unregistered, "sent by no heaven the Court oversees." She offers to seal his cultivation and let him live. He refuses: "I'm going up. I want to see where it came from." **Hook:** Skygate Pavilion announces that Elder Hu will inspect every climber at the gate in person.

### Chapter 9: Nine Thousand Nine Hundred and Ninety-Nine Steps
Hu's inspection uses a lightning-reading needle. Canglan dampens Ling Xiao's body with sword-qi at the cost of her own strength. Hu pauses over him and lets him through, and the reader should feel that this is worse than being stopped. The Climb: three thousand youths and Heaven-pressure that grows with every step. Fights break out on the first Challenge Ledges. Dumpling's strength shines, and so does Ling Xiao's gift for reading other climbers. Looking up from step 3,000, he sees the underside of Heaven for the first time: roots, stone and falling water the size of a sky. **Hook:** on the fifth Challenge Ledge, Hu Qingshan is waiting and calls him out by name.

### Chapter 10: The Spear on the Fifth Ledge
Spear against street fighter. Qingshan is better trained, more patient and completely unimpressed. Ling Xiao loses cleanly, his first real defeat. "You fight like someone who's never had to fight fair. Up there, they'll make you." He climbs anyway. The pressure squeezes a sliver of the lodged sword-thunder loose and he digests it, breaking to QC 9 at step 6,500 through pain. He passes the historical mark of step 8,000 and the scouts on the ledges go wild. **Hook:** Elder Hu's voice fills the Pillar: "There is a bolt-thief on my stair." The Pillar's formations seal. Nobody goes up or down.

### Chapter 11: Harvest
Ling Xiao fights Steward Liang and the Pavilion guards and loses. He wakes in Elder Hu's pavilion high on the Pillar, inside a refining formation that is drawing lightning out of him and into a pill furnace. Hu, courteous and frightened, explains: eighty years stuck at Foundation peak, afraid of the ninth bolt, his lifespan nearly gone. A Thunder-Womb pill would carry him through Core Formation. Hu isn't a monster, just a man who has never dared. Hu Qingshan overhears. **Hook:** Ling Xiao realizes the formation is a two-way street: it's pushing refined lightning into Hu, and Hu, at Foundation peak, is being pushed toward his threshold. He smiles. "Elder. How badly do you not want your tribulation?"

### Chapter 12: Force the Sky
The biggest gamble of Ling Xiao's life: reverse the formation and shove Hu over the edge into Core Formation, here, on the Pillar, with three thousand climbers on the stair. Qingshan cuts a formation line and loses his family name for it. Dumpling fights his way up the steps. Canglan breaks the Pillar's seal with a strike from her flawed core that nearly cracks it. Hu breaks through, screaming. **Hook:** the sky above the Skygate turns into a spiral wide enough to cover the whole southern Dust, and the first bolt has nowhere to fall but the crowded stair.

### Chapter 13: The Feast
The arc's centerpiece. Ling Xiao climbs into the tribulation and eats. Bolts one to four, light enough for a QC 9 body, and each one that goes into him is a bolt that doesn't fork into the climbers below. On the fourth he breaks into Foundation Establishment mid-tribulation, and bolts five and six are now within his appetite. Hu's Mountain-Bearing lightning gives him a stone-vein. He stops at six. Bolts seven to nine fall on Hu, who survives with a three-bolt core, ruined but alive. Then, at Foundation for the first time, Ling Xiao calls Canglan into the scorched circle and channels her lodged bolt back into her. He feels the sword-vein leave him like a tooth pulled. Her core closes whole. Warden Bai counts from the rim and doesn't interfere. **Hook:** every sect scout on the Pillar is running toward him.

### Chapter 14: A Thief Who Gives Back
Aftermath and the arc's big face-slap. The boy no Verdant sect would have looked at a month ago is fought over by five of them. Skygate Pavilion is disgraced and the Court arrests Steward Liang. Warden Bai rules that she has "no form for a thief who returns the goods." She registers him with the Court instead of sealing him and makes clear she'll keep counting. Verdant Pine takes Qingshan without a name, and Dumpling goes to the Hundred Flavors Hall. Old Yan of tiny Stormwatch makes the smallest offer and says the only thing that matters: "Our peak faces up." Ling Xiao takes it. **Hook:** Canglan, core complete, bows to him formally before she leaves for Cloudsea. "I owe you a bolt, Ling Xiao. Come up and collect it, if you can find us." She points not at the sky above the Verdant Heaven but past it, at a faint glow he hadn't noticed was a land.

### Chapter 15: The Size of the Sky
Ling Xiao's first night on the Verdant Heaven. It's bigger than the whole Dust; Thunderfall sits under one corner of its shadow. And its sky isn't empty. Through Old Yan's Star-Lens he sees lands: dozens, then hundreds, faint islands and glows, all drifting. The Verdant sects call them the Upper Heavens and argue about whether there are seven. Old Yan stopped counting at 1,400. Warden Bai visits with his file. The Court's highest seals on the Clear-Sky Bolt say it came from above the sky's ceiling, and she passes on, uneasily, a phrase from records she wasn't meant to read: above everything called Heaven are Realms that are *true*, and the rest of us are dust on their floor. **Final hook (the glimpse):** that night the Star-Lens rings on its own. Far above the highest lights, the sky opens for one heartbeat, like an eye, onto a light that isn't a star. A single thread of lightning falls from it toward some land he will never see. On the wind, faint as memory, he tastes it: the taste that has been in his body since the crater.

## Later arcs (sketch)

- **Arc Two: Verdant Heaven.** Ling Xiao is the lowest disciple of the weakest sect on a continent he once called Heaven, with a Court registration, a new name ("the Thunder-Womb") that every alchemist wants, and a stone-vein he has no idea how to use. The Verdant Grand Contest, the secret of the Star-Lens, and Scarlet Lamp's return. The Verdant sects learn that their Heaven is a lower land to someone. He goes looking for Core-weight tribulations to eat.
- **Arc Three: Cloudsea.** Core Formation: his first own tribulation, taken on the Anvil where he started. It's also the first tribulation he eats from himself. The sky-crossing to Cloudsea, which Verdant cultivators think of as the top of the world and Cloudsea thinks of as "the middle." Canglan, and the bolt she owes him.
- **Arc Four and on: the middle and high heavens.** The Scarlet Kiln, the Hanging Gardens, other Dusts under other lands, more shards of the Sundered Heaven and the treasures in them, and the Court of Thunder's Measure, where Bai's superiors keep files she can't read. Each land is bigger, older and more certain about the shape of the sky, and each is wrong.
- **Series spine:** from the Dust to the Ceiling and through it, following the taste of the Clear-Sky Bolt toward the True Realms and the question of what he is.
