# Outline: Heaven's Debtor

## Arc One: Dust and Thunder (Chapters 1–15, about 45,000 words)

**Arc question:** Can a scorch-picker who owes Heaven a bolt climb out of the Dust and live through the day it comes to collect?
**Arc shape:** From the red stakes of the Anvil to the First Heaven. Ling Xiao steals his first bolt, gets hunted by a Warden, a swordswoman and a gang, climbs the Skygate Pillar, is captured by an old elder who wants to refine him into a pill, and breaks into Foundation Establishment early to escape, calling his whole debt down on himself. He pays it, and ends the arc standing on the First Heaven with his mother breathing clean air and eight Heavens still above him.
**Clock:** The Skygate Climb opens 31 days after Ch. 1. Mei Canglan's flawed core sets permanently in about 90 days.
**Power range:** Ling Xiao goes from QC 3 to Foundation Establishment (early). Enemies range from QC 5 (gang) through QC 9 (rival, gang boss) and Foundation (steward, Elder Hu) to Core and Nascent Soul (Canglan, Scarlet Lamp, Warden Bai), who cannot be beaten, only outplayed or survived.
**Rule of thumb:** every chapter has a fight, a breakthrough or a heist.

### Chapter 1: Put It On My Tab *(drafted)*
Thunderfall at the edge of the Anvil, all nine Heavens visible overhead. Ling Xiao (QC 3, stuck two years) explains his plan to Dumpling: the Skygate token costs 100 stones, a thunder-heart sells for 300, and a sword cultivator's ninth bolt forks toward metal. He humiliates Big Tan of the Iron Crows by dropping him into hollow glass. Mei Canglan of the Cloudsea Sword Sect takes her Core Formation tribulation. In Heaven's breath before the ninth bolt, Ling Xiao sprints two hundred paces inside the stakes and plants a heaven-iron spike. The fork hits him. Heaven weighs him and he tells it: "Put it on my tab. I'll pay you at the top." It writes him down. He breaks from QC 3 to QC 6 in three breaths, banks the rest as sword-flavored thunder, and finds a black stroke on his palm and a thunder-heart in the mud. Canglan has an eight-bolt core and knows it. **Hook:** the Thunder Court Warden reads out "Nine bolts fell. The candidate received eight," and her eyes stop on him.

### Chapter 2: Eight Bolts and a Liar
Warden Bai Qiong questions the picker line. Ling Xiao lies to a Nascent Soul cultivator's face, and does it well: he says a stray fork hit his spike and knocked him flat, then shows a burned palm he has smeared with ash-mud. Her scroll reads him as QC 6, which no mortal should be. She lets him go with a look that says she's counting. Canglan says nothing at all. Home to Ma Tang, her cough and her tea stall. That night Big Tan and six Crows corner him for the thunder-heart. His first Borrowed Thunder: a cold arc of sword-lightning that cuts an iron pole in half. He wins, but half the alley saw lightning come out of his hand. **Hook:** next morning, Mei Canglan is sitting at Ma's stall drinking tea with her cracked sword across her knees. "You have something of mine."

### Chapter 3: A Sword at the Tea Stall
Canglan's bargain, told sparingly: her ninth bolt will come back down when he reaches Foundation, and if she stands in it with him she can complete her core. She has about ninety days. So she will keep him alive, and he will reach Foundation fast. The Dust's qi is too thin, so he has to get up the Skygate. Training on the Anvil means her beating him flat, over and over. At dusk he lands one touch by leading her onto hollow glass, and she almost smiles. **Hook:** the Thunder Court posts a bounty on every wall in Thunderfall: *Bolt-thief. Qi Condensation. 1,000 stones.* Every gang in town is reading it.

### Chapter 4: The Ghost Market
Ling Xiao sells the thunder-heart in the lava tubes under the Anvil to buy his Skygate token and Ma's medicine. He meets Hu Qingshan, a cold, disciplined QC 9 spear fighter who is buying a spear head and doesn't bother to hide his contempt for "picker luck." Steward Liang of Skygate Pavilion wins the heart at auction. In the tunnels afterward two rogue bounty hunters (QC 9 and Foundation early) come for the thousand-stone bounty. Ling Xiao survives the first on nerve and terrain. Canglan kills the second at the last possible moment ("You were doing fine. Barely."). **Hook:** Liang holds the heart to the lamp and smiles. There's fresh-bolt qi in it, and a trace of something no thunder-heart should carry: the signature of a debtor. He sends word up the Pillar to Elder Hu.

### Chapter 5: Blood Lightning in the Ruin
Canglan says QC 6 will not survive the upper Skygate. Ling Xiao's answer is to steal more bolts. She points out that he'll die at Foundation. He says then he'll be stronger before he dies. Dumpling's uncle, a smuggler, knows of an illegal tribulation in the Ruin, the wreck of the Fourth Heaven: Daoist Scarlet Lamp of the Blood Lamp Sect, going for Core. They cross 300 li of Dust and slip past the Blood Lamp guards (fight). Ling Xiao eats the sixth and seventh bolts, blood-red and hungry. QC 7, three strokes on his palm, and banked blood-thunder that wants to eat *him*. Scarlet Lamp survives with a flawed core. **Hook:** sheltering in a collapsed palace, Ling Xiao's torch finds a carving on the wall of a man holding lightning, with Ling Xiao's Ledger mark on his palm. The inscription reads *Debtor*.

### Chapter 6: What the Fallen Heaven Owed
Scarlet Lamp, now Core Formation and enraged, hunts them through the Ruin. There are qi-warped beasts in broken palaces, and a chase across an upside-down city. Ling Xiao learns to control blood-thunder by feeding it once and then refusing it. Canglan takes a wound protecting him and hates that she did. They trade insults like two people becoming friends. They escape with one rubbing of the carving. **Hook:** back in Thunderfall, Ma's stall is smashed, a crow is painted on the shutters, and Dumpling is gone.

### Chapter 7: Crow's Nest
A raid on the Iron Crows' old kiln fortress. The Big Tan rematch lasts three moves, which shows the reader how far Ling Xiao has come since Ch. 1. Then Crow-Father Gou (QC 9, iron claws): a hard, ugly fight in a burning kiln. Ling Xiao burns nearly all his sword-thunder to win and breaks to QC 8 mid-fight. Dumpling is rescued and Ma is safe with the neighbors. In Gou's office are payment slips stamped with Steward Liang's seal: Skygate Pavilion has been paying the Crows. **Hook:** Warden Bai steps out of the smoke.

### Chapter 8: The Warden's Arithmetic
A chase over the rooftops and onto the Anvil, a Nascent Soul against QC 8. He can't win or outrun her, so he outthinks her. He runs into the red circle of a *sanctioned* tribulation in progress, where the Court's own law forbids her to interfere, and survives the stray lightning on the edge. From the stakes, she tells him what the Court believes: an unpaid debt cracked the Fourth Heaven. She offers to seal his cultivation forever and let him live. He refuses: "I'm going up." She says she'll be there to count when Heaven collects. **Hook:** Skygate Pavilion announces that Elder Hu will inspect every climber at the gate in person, palms included.

### Chapter 9: Nine Thousand Nine Hundred and Ninety-Nine Steps
Canglan hides the Ledger mark under a fresh sword cut. Elder Hu inspects his palm, pauses, and lets him through. The reader should feel that this is worse than being stopped. The Climb: three thousand youths and Heaven-pressure that grows with every step. Fights break out on the first Challenge Ledges. Dumpling's strength shines, and so does Ling Xiao's gift for reading other climbers. He spends blood-thunder to push through the pressure and pays for it. The scouts start watching. **Hook:** on the fifth Challenge Ledge, Hu Qingshan is waiting and calls him out by name.

### Chapter 10: The Spear on the Fifth Ledge
Spear against street fighter. Qingshan is better trained, more patient and completely unimpressed. Ling Xiao loses cleanly, his first real defeat. "You fight like someone who's never had to fight fair. Up there, they'll make you." Ling Xiao gets up and climbs anyway. Grinding against the pressure, he breaks to QC 9 at step 6,500 through pain rather than lightning. He passes the historical mark of step 8,000 and the scouts on the ledges go wild. **Hook:** Elder Hu's voice fills the Pillar: "There is a bolt-thief on my stair." The Pillar's formations seal. Nobody goes up or down.

### Chapter 11: Harvest
Ling Xiao fights Steward Liang and the Pavilion guards and loses. He wakes in Elder Hu's pavilion high on the Pillar, inside a refining formation that is drawing the banked thunder out of him. Hu, courteous and frightened, explains: eighty years stuck at Foundation peak, afraid of the ninth bolt, and his lifespan nearly gone. A debtor's lightning refined into a Thunder-Debt Pill would carry him through Core Formation. Hu is not a monster, just a man who has taken all his life and never paid. Hu Qingshan overhears. **Hook:** the formation starts pulling at the *owed* bolts. The Ledger mark bleeds ink, and the sky above the Pillar darkens. Heaven has noticed someone touching its accounts.

### Chapter 12: Collect Early
The biggest gamble of Ling Xiao's life: the only way out of the formation is up. He will break into Foundation now, inside Hu's formation, and call his whole debt down on the Pillar. Qingshan cuts a formation line and loses his family name for it. Dumpling fights his way up the steps. Canglan breaks the Pillar's seal with a strike from her flawed core that nearly cracks it. Ling Xiao pushes through into Foundation. **Hook:** the sky above the Skygate turns into a spiral the width of three provinces. The whole southern Dust looks up.

### Chapter 13: Heaven Collects
The tribulation is the arc's centerpiece. Three Core Formation bolts return at full strength to a boy on the threshold of Foundation. Each has its own flavor and its own lesson. Elder Hu, desperate, tries to seize the second bolt for himself. Heaven weighs a man who took all his life and never paid, and he is struck to ash. For the last bolt, Canglan's sword-bolt, she steps into the circle and they take it together, back to back. Warden Bai counts from the rim and does not interfere. Ling Xiao lives. His palm is clean, his body is tempered by all three bolts, and sword and blood thunder settle into him as paid imprints. Foundation Establishment. Canglan's core is whole. **Hook:** every sect scout on the Pillar is running toward him.

### Chapter 14: A Debtor Who Pays
Aftermath and the arc's big face-slap. The boy no First Heaven sect would have looked at a month ago is fought over by five of them. Skygate Pavilion is disgraced and the Court arrests Steward Liang. Warden Bai rules: "A debtor who pays is not a thief." She registers him with the Court instead of sealing him and makes clear she'll keep counting. Verdant Pine takes Qingshan without a name, and Dumpling goes to the Hundred Flavors Hall. Old Yan of the tiny Stormwatch Sect makes the smallest offer and says the only thing that matters: "Our peak faces up." Ling Xiao takes it. **Hook:** Canglan, core complete, bows to him formally. "Cloudsea repays its debts. I owe you a bolt, Ling Xiao. One day I will pay it." It's a promise and a challenge.

### Chapter 15: The Address at the Top
Ma Tang steps onto the First Heaven and takes her first breath of clean air. She laughs, coughs less, and starts arguing with Stormwatch's cook. Ling Xiao sits on the rim of the Stormwatch peak with the Anvil far below and eight Heavens above. Warden Bai visits with a quiet warning: the Court's records say the last Debtor paid everything as well, all the way up, and then the Fourth Heaven fell. **Final hook:** that night a new stroke appears on his palm, though he has stolen nothing. It's red, not black. Heaven has extended him credit. In the rubbing from the Ruin, the Debtor's hand carries one red stroke too.

## Later arcs (sketch, not for this meeting)

- **Arc Two: Verdant Heaven.** Ling Xiao is the lowest disciple of the weakest sect on the First Heaven, and he has a Court registration, a red stroke and enemies. Skygate Pavilion wants revenge. The First Heaven's Grand Contest, the Stormwatch Sect's secret, and Scarlet Lamp's return. The red stroke calls for a bolt he hasn't stolen yet.
- **Arc Three: Cloudsea.** Core Formation. His first *real* tribulation, nine bolts plus the debt, taken on the Anvil where he started. The Second Heaven, Canglan's sect, and the bolt she owes him.
- **Series spine:** one Heaven per arc or two, each bigger and more dangerous, climbing toward the Ninth, Lingxiao Hall, and whoever keeps the Ledger.
