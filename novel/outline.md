# Outline: The Pill Taster

## Arc One: The Dregs Pit (Chapters 1–15, about 45,000 words)

**Arc question:** Who poisoned the Nine-Turn Marrow Pill, and can a dead taster prove it?
**Arc shape:** Shen Mo goes from disposable taster to a recognized, if distrusted, member of the sect who has exposed a poisoner, and he learns the scandal goes higher than one man.
**Power range:** Shen Mo rises from Qi Condensation layer 1 to layer 6. Antagonists are Foundation Establishment. Elders are offstage threats.

### Chapter 1: The Taster *(drafted)*
The Tasting Hall at dawn: Shen Mo, his ledger, the other tasters. Steward Kang assigns Lin Qiao's new Foundation-grade Nine-Turn Marrow Pill, meant for Senior Brother Pei's breakthrough in ten days, to twelve-year-old Xu Ping. Shen Mo talks his way into taking it instead. Elder Gu Hanshan watches. Shen Mo names every ingredient, then tastes something that isn't in the formula: the snuffed-wick bitterness of Grave-Lotus ash. At the fifth turn his heart stops. Gu blames the silting, orders the rest of the batch tested on Xu Ping at first bell, and has the body sent to the pit. Shen Mo wakes in the Dregs Pit. Nine years of dregs have become a grey seed in his dantian, and blind Granny Fu is watching him. He writes Entry 3,962 and starts to climb. **Hook:** four hours to first bell, and he is legally dead.

### Chapter 2: Dead Men Don't Knock
Climbing out of the pit using the seed's new strength and what he knows about the slag. He sneaks back through the Pill Peak drainage channels. At first bell he can't reveal himself, so he switches the tray pill for a harmless Spleen-Warming pill of matching colour, which he learned to do years ago to protect sick tasters. Xu Ping survives the "reduced dose" with confusing readings. Gu is intrigued. **Hook:** Lin Qiao catches Shen Mo in the drain with the real pill in his hand.

### Chapter 3: The Alchemist's Debt
A tense bargain. Lin Qiao believes him only when he recites her formula to the gram, including her private change to the lotus seed. Together they confirm Grave-Lotus ash in the batch. It exists only in the sealed stores, so she is the obvious suspect. She hides his survival; he helps her find the poisoner. **Hook:** the sealed-store register shows no Grave-Lotus withdrawals in ten years.

### Chapter 4: Name Before You Eat
Granny Fu's first (reluctant) lesson. Shen Mo learns the two rules the hard way: an unnamed dreg rebels and tears a meridian, and a named one refines but makes him relive the pill. He refines the gentle dregs first (Spleen-Warming, Bone-Knitting Paste) and reaches layer 3. Show the cost, don't lecture. **Hook:** Granny Fu smells Grave-Lotus on him and goes white. "Where did you eat that?"

### Chapter 5: Senior Brother Pei
Pei Wenyuan visits the Tasting Hall to thank the tasters, which nobody has ever done, and meets Shen Mo in disguise as a pit-sifter. Pei is kind and clueless. Shen Mo realizes the poison was aimed at Pei and that a clean batch is still being refined for him. **Hook:** Pei's breakthrough is moved up to three days from now.

### Chapter 6: The Steward's Ledger
Shen Mo breaks into Steward Kang's quarters. Kang is not the poisoner, but he sells tester rosters and falsifies deaths: 41 "reassignments" in two years that were actually deaths. Shen Mo copies the pages. **Hook:** Kang returns early. Shen Mo escapes, but drops a page with his handwriting on it.

### Chapter 7: The Dead Taster Returns
Shen Mo stops hiding. He walks into the Tasting Hall in daylight and tells the story that he woke in the pit. Kang tries to have him beaten as a deserter. Shen Mo quietly mentions the number 41 in front of an Outer Affairs clerk. First face-slap: earned, quiet, and costly (Kang is now an enemy). **Hook:** Elder Gu summons him. "The dead should not have a pulse. Show me yours."

### Chapter 8: Specimen
Gu examines him with needles, pulse-plates and a Clearing bath that nearly strips the seed. Shen Mo hides the seed by refining a dreg mid-examination and faking silting, at real cost. Gu reassigns him as his personal taster, with better pay and far more danger. **Hook:** Gu hands him a pill to taste, and it contains a trace of Grave-Lotus. Gu is testing *him*.

### Chapter 9: The Market Under the Bridge
Shen Mo and Xu Ping go to the outer market with Shen Mo's first real contribution points. He meets Tang Yu, a rogue dregs-merchant who sells failed pills for their residue, a new supply for his cultivation. Lighter chapter: breathing room, Xu Ping's humor, the world beyond the sect. Tang Yu mentions a buyer of Grave-Lotus from the Thousand Waves Sect (red herring). Shen Mo reaches layer 4. **Hook:** Xu Ping is chosen for the outer-disciple trial in the Old Furnace, and so is Shen Mo.

### Chapter 10: Pei's Breakthrough
Before the trial, Pei's Foundation Establishment ceremony. Lin Qiao has refined a clean batch, and Shen Mo tasted it that morning. Pei succeeds, and the sect celebrates. Shen Mo watches from the tasters' gallery and sees Zhou Heng's face at the moment of success: not disappointment but calculation. **Hook:** Zhou Heng is appointed warden of the Old Furnace trial.

*(Pacing note to self: this middle stretch is where my drafts usually sag. Chapters 9–10 must each move the mystery forward by one concrete clue, not just add texture.)*

### Chapter 11: The Old Furnace
The trial. Twenty outer disciples enter the sealed furnace, a small secret realm of ash-corridors, dormant fire formations and pill-beasts born from ancient spilled elixirs. Shen Mo leads with knowledge rather than strength and eats beast-dregs he can name. Rivalry and alliances among the disciples. **Hook:** the formations reactivate early, and someone sealed the exit.

### Chapter 12: The Founders' Wall
Deep in the furnace, Shen Mo and Xu Ping find the founders' chamber: carvings showing the Azure Furnace was founded by tasters, and the first lines of the Hundred Dregs Sutra. Shen Mo understands what Granny Fu is. Xu Ping is badly hurt by a fire formation. **Hook:** Zhou Heng is waiting at the chamber door. He sealed the exit.

### Chapter 13: Ash Against Foundation
Shen Mo (layer 5) against Zhou Heng (Foundation, late). He cannot win on power. He wins on knowledge: Zhou's body is silted with dregs from pills Shen Mo tasted, so Shen Mo knows exactly which meridians are weak, and he uses the furnace's dormant formations. He does not kill Zhou. He needs him alive. Breakthrough to layer 6 mid-fight, paid for by reliving a lethal pill. **Hook:** they emerge carrying Zhou Heng, and the Law Hall arrests *Shen Mo*.

### Chapter 14: Trial by Taste
Law Hall tribunal. Kang testifies against Shen Mo. Lin Qiao risks her standing to back him. Shen Mo proposes the only proof he has: blindfolded before the elders, he tastes a dozen pills and names every ingredient, including hidden Grave-Lotus in the sample Zhou handled. Public vindication and the big face-slap of the arc, earned by nine years of work. Zhou Heng begins to name who ordered him and dies mid-sentence of Grave-Lotus poisoning. **Hook:** only three people had access to Zhou's cell.

### Chapter 15: Foundation of Ash
Aftermath. Kang is demoted (the 41 deaths are exposed). Shen Mo is made an official inner-sect taster under Lin Qiao, a real post with Clearing rights, which he now doesn't need. Xu Ping recovers. Pei offers his friendship, uneasy now that he knows what his pills cost. Granny Fu's hut is empty. **Final hook:** Elder Gu, privately, tells Shen Mo the Sect Master takes one pill every day that no one has ever been permitted to taste, and asks him to taste the next one.
